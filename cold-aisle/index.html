          How to play

          • Reboot every infected rack of the floor (hold E or Space while facing it), then use the lift.
          • W and S or Up and Down move, A and D strafe, the mouse or Left and Right turn.
          • Fire: the left mouse button, J or Enter. Tools: 1 to 3 or the wheel. Use doors and the lift with E or Space.
          • Tab shows the map, P or Esc pause, F is fullscreen, M mutes.

          How to play: the goal, the keys, tools, enemies, items, racks and the lift, upgrades

          Goal

          A worm took over the building automation of the data centre. On each of the floors, find the infected racks and reboot them by hand, then take the lift down. The last floor is the chiller plant, and its rogue chiller is the boss. Between floors you spend patches on upgrades.

          Keys

          Captured by Click to play, the mouse turns the view freely; Esc gives it back and pauses, as do switching windows and hiding the tab. Where the browser will not capture it, the mouse turns the view while it is over the frame, and resting it at the left or right edge keeps turning.

          Tools

          • 1 Screwdriver: close up only ( tiles), damage every s. It needs no ammo, and a tool that runs dry falls back to it.
          • 2 Tone probe: hits the first thing in line, damage every s, one cell a shot. You carry up to cells.
          • 3 Degausser: rays of in a degree fan every s, weaker past tiles, one coil a shot. You carry up to coils.

          Every shot is heard: sleeping machines nearby wake up.

          Enemies and their warning signs

          Every attack shows before it lands, and every shot can be sidestepped. A machine wakes when it sees you, hears a shot or is hit.

          • Floor scrubber ( HP) rolls at you and strikes for . Sign: it rears up for s (right) before it strikes. Step back.
          • Inventory drone ( HP) keeps its distance and shoots sparks of . Sign: the spark charges for s. Strafe.
          • Camera turret ( HP) never moves. Sign: it locks on for s, then fires bolts of . Break its line of sight behind a wall.
          • Pallet loader ( HP) charges in a straight line for . Sign: it flashes for s before the charge. Step aside; after a charge it stands stunned for s.
          • The chiller ( HP), the boss of the last floor: it fires fans of frost bolts, calls floor scrubbers (at most at a time) and only takes damage while its vents are open (right). Its bolts stop only at walls: keep one between you and it.

          Items

          Walk over an item to take it. One that would do nothing (health already full) stays where it is.

          • Cells and coils: cells for the probe, coils for the degausser. The tools themselves come with cells and coils.
          • Coffee and medkit: and health.
          • ESD vest: armour.
          • Badges: blue, yellow and red, for the doors of their colour.
          • Patch: the currency of upgrades. Some wait behind secret walls.

          Racks, secrets and the lift

          • Infected rack: face it and hold Use for s; a bar fills, and the rack turns green. Letting go or turning away stops the reboot.
          • Secret wall: concrete with a small tell. Use it and it opens for good.
          • Lift: locked until every infected rack of the floor is clean (and, on the last floor, the chiller is down). Use it to end the floor.

          The flashlight lights tiles ahead; dark rooms are only a look, the machines see you just the same.
