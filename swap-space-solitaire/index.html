How to play
  • Get every suit from ace to king onto its disk: /dev/sda spades, sdb hearts, sdc diamonds, sdd clubs.
  • In a cascade a card goes on the next higher card of the other colour; any card or run may go to an empty cascade.
  • A swap slot parks one card. A run of cards moves only while it is no longer than (free swap slots + 1) × 2 for every empty cascade.
  • Tap a card, then tap where it goes. Tap the picked card again to send it to its disk, or else to the first free swap slot. Cards no longer needed go to the disks by themselves.
  • Every move is a page fault. Undo takes back the last one (up to 500); Restart, pressed twice, goes back to the first layout and counts the moves it takes back as undos. The clock runs from your first move.
  • Keys, with the focus on the game: 1–8 cascades, Q W E R swap slots, F the disk, U or Ctrl+Z undo, Esc cancel.
  • Every deal number replays the same deal; the daily deal is the same for everyone and scores once a day. Nearly every deal can be solved, but none is guaranteed.
