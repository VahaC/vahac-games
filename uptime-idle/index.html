    How to play
    • Services earn credits every second and take one slot each. The old laptop has 2 slots; a mini PC, a NAS or a virtualization node adds more.
    • Every machine takes a place: the desk holds 8. A server rack holds 16 more. A machine whose power would eat all of the income is refused: buy a service first.
    • Each copy of an item costs 15% more than the one before.
    • The power bill follows the usual formula, watts × hours × price per kWh / 1000 (the one the Homelab Power Cost Calculator uses): each second of play is billed as one hour at 4 cr per kWh, times the PUE of your cooling. Better cooling lowers the PUE.
    • Incidents: a failed disk halves the income until you replace it (a RAID mirror makes it harmless), a power outage stops everything for 30 s (a UPS rides it out), and a kernel update gives +50% income for 60 s if you reboot in time.
    • The session goal asks for about 3 minutes of income and pays a bonus of 90 seconds of it, so a short visit always gets somewhere.
    • Closed, hidden or asleep, the lab keeps earning at its net income for up to 8 hours. A clock set backwards counts nothing until it is past the time of the last save again.
    • With a server rack and 300.0M cr earned in a run you can migrate to a new rack: everything starts over, and the run's earnings become rack points, +2% income each, for good.
    • Keyboard, with the focus in the game: 1 to 9 buy the services and hardware in shop order. Tab and Enter reach every button.
