      How to play
      • Each hole has lines to catch and lines to leave, taken from logs and configs. Write one regular expression that matches somewhere in every line under Catch and in none under Leave.
      • Every character is a stroke. Par is the length of a good solution: fewer strokes is a birdie (-1) or an eagle (-2), more is a bogey (+1).
      • The lines turn green or red while you type, and the part your pattern matches is marked. An invalid pattern shows the engine's error and nothing else happens.
      • Enter, or Hole out, scores the pattern. The first pattern that holes a hole goes on this round's card; a shorter one later still sets the hole's best. Prev, Next and the card move between holes, and every hole keeps its draft.
      • Complete all 18 holes for a round total. New round clears the card and the drafts and keeps your bests.
      • On a phone the chips just above the pattern box insert the tokens the keyboard hides, at the caret, and the short count just above the box shows how many lines pass while the keyboard is open.
      • Patterns run in a Web Worker that is stopped after 300 ms, so a runaway pattern such as (a+)+$ cannot freeze the page. Where the page cannot start a worker, patterns run on the page itself: up to 60 characters, and a pattern that could take more than 1,500,000 backtracking steps on a line, such as (a+)+, (a|a)* or (.?){20}, is refused before it runs. The line under the buttons says which mode is on.
      • Want to see why a pattern matches? Paste it into the Regex Tester in the Tools section.
