Wrap board: opposite edges join. A cable leaving the right edge comes back in on the left of the same row, and the top edge joins the bottom one. Marks on the rim show each crossing: cyan where cables meet across the edge, green once that link reaches the switch, red in a loop, amber or grey where a cable gets no answer, and faint where no cable crosses (that is fine). The tile you last touched and the tile across the edge from it get a dashed outline.

How to play
  • Tap or click a tile to turn it a quarter turn clockwise. Connect every server, NAS and access point to the core switch in the middle.
  • Cables reached from the switch light up green (link up) and carry packets out from the switch. A closed loop glows red: a broadcast storm. Amber tips mark cable ends nobody answers.
  • Right-click a tile, or switch on Lock mode and tap it, to lock a tile you are sure about.
  • Keyboard, with the board focused: arrows move the cursor, Space or Enter turn clockwise, Q turns back, L locks, N starts a new game.
  • Target is the number of rotations the generated solution needs. Other solutions can exist, so you may beat it.
  • Restart puts the same board back to its scramble. Once you have turned a tile, that run is a replay and sets no best: New Game deals a scored board.
  • Wrap boards join opposite edges, like a torus: a cable leaving the right edge enters the left tile of the same row, and top joins bottom the same way. Marks on the rim show each crossing, and a tile on the rim and the tile across the edge from it share a dashed outline when you touch one of them.
  • Each win offers the next rung of the ladder: 5x5, 7x7, 9x9, then the same with wrap.
  • The Daily is the same 7x7 board for everyone, one scored attempt per day, and its clock runs while it is on screen. Solve it on consecutive days to grow your streak.
