    How to play

    • Malware walks in from the right along the lanes. Each lane has one breaker at the rack: the first enemy to reach the rack trips it and a surge wipes that lane; the next one there is a breach and ends the level.
    • Cycles pay for defenders. Each level starts you with its own amount, more trickles in over time (not in the Hot aisle), and PSUs and UPSes make more. A card recharges after each use.
    • Mouse or touch: pick a card, then a floor cell. Picking the same card again puts it down. Decommission removes a defender, with no refund.
    • Keyboard, with the field focused (click it or Tab to it): 1 to 9 and 0 pick a card (0 is the tenth), the arrows move the cell cursor, Enter or Space places, X or Delete picks Decommission, P pauses (Esc too, outside fullscreen), S switches between 1x and 2x speed, F toggles fullscreen.
    • Fullscreen (F or the button) gives the biggest field. On a phone, turn it sideways first.
    • A level is won when every enemy is gone: 3 stars with no breaker tripped, 2 with one or two, 1 with more. Winning unlocks new defenders and more card slots; the Codex lists everything you have seen.
    • From zone 6 on every zone has a rule of its own: express lanes, hot cells, power rails, tunnel exits, blackouts, crossovers, upkeep, rolling outages and wider fog; the last zone mixes two of them. The badges over the field show the level's rules and what they do next: tap one for its full line. Blackouts and outages are announced before they strike; in a blackout only what your shooters can see stays lit.
    • The game pauses when the tab is hidden or the window loses the focus. Progress is saved after each level, and a level in progress is saved too: when you pause, leave it for the level select, hide the tab or close the page, and every half minute of play. The level select then offers to resume it; starting any level, winning or losing ends that save.
