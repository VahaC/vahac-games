How to play
  • The read head bounces off the walls and the carriage at the bottom. Where it lands on the carriage sets the angle: straight up in the middle, steeper toward the ends.
  • Plain sectors break with one hit, bad sectors (amber and red, with their LBA) take two or three, and read-only sectors (grey, striped) never break. A broken sector flashes its LBA address.
  • Some sectors drop a bonus capsule: RAID1 adds a mirrored second head, ECC widens the carriage for 12 s and SLOW-SEEK (SLOW on its capsule) slows the heads for 10 s. Catch it with the carriage.
  • A head that falls past the carriage is a reallocated sector on the SMART readout. The third one fails the drive. A track with no sector left is a complete surface scan, and the next track has more rows and faster heads.
  • Mouse: the carriage follows the pointer. Touch: drag anywhere on the platter, for example in the strip below the carriage, and the carriage moves as far as your finger. A tap or a click launches the head.
  • Keyboard, with the platter focused (click it or Tab to it): Left and Right or A and D move the carriage, Space launches, P pauses. The game also pauses when the tab is hidden, and a game you come back to (from the other view or after a reload) waits paused with its head where it was.
  • The Daily disk is the same track for everyone, one scored attempt per day. Clear it on consecutive days to grow your streak, and replay it unscored with Practice.
