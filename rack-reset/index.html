How to play
  • Tap or click an LED to press it. A press flips that LED and the neighbours its rack model's toggle mask covers: lit alarms go out, clear ones light up.
  • The mask is a 3x3 stencil in three hex digits, one per row from the top (4 left, 2 centre, 1 right): 0x272 is a cross, 0x525 a diagonal X, 0x070 a row of three, 0x575 an H, 0x632 a hook and 0x777 the full block. The small grid above the rack shows it.
  • Each row of LEDs is also a register in hex, the left LED its highest bit. The rack is reset when every register reads 0.
  • Par is the true minimum, worked out by a GF(2) solver built into the page: Gaussian elimination plus a check of every solution its null space allows. Pressing an LED twice cancels out, so the order of presses never matters.
  • Undo takes back the last press, Restart goes back to the rack's first state, and Hint shows one LED of a fewest-press reset from where you are. A rack where you used a hint, or that you restart after resetting it, is not scored.
  • New Game, N or a new model or size deal a new rack at once when the rack in play has no presses or is reset. With presses on it, a new model or size waits for New Game, and New Game or N has to be pressed a second time within 4 s, so one slip never throws a rack away. A dealt rack always needs at least 3 presses on 3x3 and 4 on larger racks.
  • Best is your fewest presses over par for each model and size ("par" when you matched it), and Cleared counts your scored resets.
  • Keyboard, with the rack focused: arrows move the cursor, Space or Enter press, H hint, U undo, R restart, N new game.
