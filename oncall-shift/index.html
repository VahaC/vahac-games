        How to play
        • Every incident needs one action: cleanup a full disk, restart a memory leak or a lost connection, scale a CPU at 100%, renew an expiring certificate.
        • Tap an action, then the servers to fix (the action stays armed), or tap a server first and then its action.
        • When the SLA bar runs out the server goes down and counts downtime until it gets the right action. A wrong action adds 4 minutes of downtime.
        • A down database, cache, queue or DNS server knocks out the servers that depend on it: fix the root cause first.
        • Keyboard (with the rack focused): arrows move, 1 to 4 choose an action, Enter applies it, Space pauses, Escape clears.
        • Uptime is graded in nines: 99% is two nines, 99.9% three, 99.999% five. The shift pauses when you switch tabs.
        • Level sets the difficulty of your next shift: a shift under way plays on at its own level until you press New Shift.
