How to play

  • Write your bit next to the other side's bits so a straight line of them is trapped between the new bit and one of yours: the whole line flips.
  • A side without a legal move passes. When neither side can move, the most bits win.
  • Keys on the board: ←↑→↓ move, Enter or Space write, U undo, N new game.
