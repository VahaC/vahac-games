      How to play: keys, the top bar, capabilities, enemies and the boss

      Goal

      You are a process that was just forked, and your parent process has died: you are an orphan. You walk through a Linux file system looking for PID 1, the first process, which adopts orphans. Episode 1 covers /home and /tmp: 15 rooms, two capabilities and one boss, tmpfiles.d. It ends when you take the boss’s reward, with a screen of your time, deaths, man pages and completion.

      Controls

      The game reads the keyboard only while it has the focus: click the game, or Click to play. It pauses when it loses the focus or the tab is hidden.

      KeysWhat they do
      Left and Right, A and DMove.
      Up, W or SpaceJump. Hold the key to jump higher; let go early for a short hop.
      Down and jumpDrop through a one-way platform you stand on.
      X or JSend a signal. Needs the kill capability.
      C or KDash. Needs the renice capability.
      MOpen or close the map. The game stands still while it is open.
      P or EscPause and resume.
      FFullscreen on and off.
      EnterClose a message.

      Until you own a capability, its keys only show “Operation not permitted”.

      The top bar, left to right

      • RSS is your health (in Linux, RSS is the memory a process holds). You start with 5 chips. A hit or spikes cost one chip, and then you blink for 1 s and cannot be hurt. A tty refills all of them, a chip item refills one. At 0 the process is killed and restarts at the last tty, keeping everything it collected.
      • CPU is your energy. A signal costs 10 CPU and a dash 30 CPU. It refills by 20 a second, up to its maximum (100 at the start).
      • NICE is your level, and the thin bar after it is your XP toward the next one. In Linux the nice value is a process’s priority, from 19 (least important) to -20. You start at nice 19 and go one down with every level. It is not the dash: the dash is the renice capability.
      • MAN counts the man pages you found, out of 12.
      • SWEEP shows only in /tmp. The bar empties over 20 seconds; in its last 1 s the temporary crates blink, then they vanish for 3 s (the bar turns red) and come back.
      • PID is your process id. It goes up by one after every death.
      • Area is /HOME or /TMP.
      • Boss bar at the bottom of the screen shows TMPFILES.D and its HP while the boss is awake.

      Capabilities

      • kill lies on a pedestal in /home/Documents. X or J sends a signal straight ahead for 8 tiles. It costs 10 CPU, and you can send one every 0.25 s. It hurts bugs, daemons, turrets and the boss’s open core, flips signal switches and stops at walls. It passes through zombies.
      • renice is the reward for beating the boss of /tmp. C or K dashes 4 tiles in 0.2 s the way you face, without falling. It costs 30 CPU, and the next dash can start 0.5 s after the last one started. It breaks cracked walls for good, and a jump followed by a dash crosses a gap of 5 tiles that a jump alone cannot. renice is the Linux command that changes the priority of a running process; owning it does not change your NICE level.

      Things in the world

      • tty (a terminal) is a checkpoint. Touch it: your progress is saved, RSS is refilled and its screen lights. After a death, or after Back to last tty, you restart at the last tty you touched (or at the start if there is none).
      • Man page gives 10 XP and one true sentence about Linux. There are 12; the pause screen lists the ones you found.
      • Chip heals 1 RSS. Every third enemy you defeat drops one.
      • Memory module adds 1 max RSS for good. Two are hidden in the world.
      • Signal switch and gate: hit the switch with a signal. It stays on and opens the gate in the same room.
      • r-- door is read-only for you: it needs setuid, which no process has in this episode. It cannot be opened; touching it says Permission denied.
      • Cracked wall breaks only under a renice dash.
      • One-way platform: jump up through it and land on top; Down and jump drop through.
      • Crate and temporary crate: a crate is solid. A temporary crate vanishes during the sweep in /tmp and in the boss fight; if it comes back where you stand, it lifts you on top.
      • Crumbling tile falls 0.4 s after you land on it and comes back 3 s later.
      • Spikes (a segfault) cost 1 RSS and put you back on the last safe ground.

      Enemies

      Touching an enemy or a shot costs 1 RSS. Before you have kill you cannot hurt anything: jump over or go around. Defeated enemies come back when you enter the room again.

      • Bug: 1 HP, 5 XP. Walks the floor and turns at walls and ledges. Jump over it, or send one signal.
      • Leaky daemon (a daemon is a background process): 3 HP, 15 XP. Stands still and, every 3 s while you are near, spawns a zombie, at most 3 alive at once. Three signals; when it dies, all its zombies are reaped.
      • Zombie process (a process that has ended but whose parent never collected it): floats slowly toward you, through walls, when you are within 8 tiles. Signals pass through it, so it cannot be killed. Kill its daemon and it is reaped for 2 XP.
      • Lockfile turret and its shot: 2 HP, 10 XP. Faces you and fires a slow straight shot every 2 s; the shot stops at walls and cannot be hit. Jump over the shots; two signals destroy the turret.

      The boss, tmpfiles.d

      • tmpfiles.d (the service that cleans /tmp) sleeps at the far end of its arena until you are 5 tiles in. Then it wakes, and a gate shuts the door behind you until it is down. It has 12 HP in three phases of 4, and each phase is faster. Left: closed; right: its core open.
      1. Wind-up: it shakes. Get ready to jump, or go up to a platform.
      2. Sweep: it slides along the floor to the other end. Be up on a one-way platform, or jump over it.
      3. Warning: the temporary crates blink.
      4. Delete: the crates vanish for 3.5 s while temp files hang under the ceiling and then fall: one over you, the others 5 columns apart. Step away from the one over you; a platform above you stops them.
      5. Core open for 2 s: shoot it. Only the open core takes damage, at most one hit every 0.7 s. The hit that ends a phase closes it, and it rages for 1 s.

      Winning gives 40 XP, opens the gate and leaves the renice capability on a pedestal; taking it ends the episode 2.5 s later. If you die, you restart at the tty just before the arena, and the fight starts again with the boss at full HP. To leave in the middle of the fight, pause (P or Esc) and press Back to last tty.

      Levels and XP

      XP comes from defeated enemies and from man pages. The first level needs 20 XP, and each next one 10 more than the one before. Every level adds 10 max CPU; every second level also adds 1 max RSS and refills RSS. A level shows a message such as “nice 18: Max CPU 110.” Enemies come back when you enter a room again, so you can always earn more XP; man pages and items stay collected.

      Map, pause and saving

      M shows the map: the rooms by area, the ones you visited lit, the current one marked, and your completion. The pause screen lists the man pages you found and has two buttons: Resume, and Back to last tty, which restarts you at the last tty with full RSS, keeps everything you collected and does not count as a death.

      Your progress is saved in this browser the moment it changes, and also when you pause, leave the game or hide the tab. Your position is not saved: after a reload, Continue resumes at the last tty with everything you collected. New game over a saved game asks for a second press within 4 s.

      Secrets

      Some cracked walls back in /home open once you have renice. It is worth going back.
